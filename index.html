About

I'm Chun Yin, a visual development artist and animator from Hong Kong, now based in Toronto.

I design characters, paint backgrounds, board stories and make motion. My work moves between illustration, vis dev and animation, and I like it best when a still image already feels like it's about to move.

Tools: Photoshop, Procreate, Toon Boom Harmony, After Effects, Blender.
