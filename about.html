About

Hi! I'm Alvin Chan, a Toronto-based visual development artist and illustrator originally from Hong Kong.

I graduated from the Savannah College of Art and Design (SCAD) with a BFA in Illustration, focusing on concept design for animation and games.

My experience includes 2D pre-production, layout, and animation background art.

I've also illustrated children's books, including How to Fly with Alaska Chai and How to Wander (And Why!) with Alaska Chai.
